About SRP Exim

A proprietorship exporter from Gandhidham, Kachchh, Gujarat, shipping fresh Bhagwa pomegranates and salt from Kutch.

Our story

SRP EXIM is a proprietorship firm based at Padana, Gandhidham, in Kachchh, Gujarat. The proprietor is Shambhubhai Sakhayabhai Humbal.

We export fresh Bhagwa pomegranates and salt. We buy directly from pomegranate farms and from the salt pans of Kutch, check the quality before dispatch, and prepare the complete export documentation.

Our office is close to Kandla (Deendayal) and Mundra ports. We work on FOB and CIF terms and serve buyers who need full container loads.
